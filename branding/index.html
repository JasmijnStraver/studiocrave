Branding

Branding for women who are ready to grow.

Logo design gives you a mark. Visual identity gives you a system. Branding is neither — it's the strategy that decides what that mark and that system should say in the first place.

Studio Crave works from strategy first. Before a single colour is chosen, we know who your brand is for, what it promises, and what makes it different from the ten other options your ideal client is comparing you to.

The result is The Branding Kitchen in practice: a complete branding solution delivered through the 7 Course Branding Experience, from the first strategy conversation to the day the new brand goes live.

Complete branding

Three pieces, one process.

Brand Strategy

Positioning, audience and messaging — the decisions everything else is built on.

Visual Identity

Logo, colour, typography and a system that presents the strategy properly.

Branding Photography

Imagery that puts the strategy and the identity in front of a camera.

Branding for female entrepreneurs works best as one process, not three separate purchases.
