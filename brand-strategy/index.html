Brand Strategy

Build the brand behind the business.

Brand strategy is the thinking that happens before anything gets designed: who you're for, what you're promising, and why someone should choose you over every other option they're considering.

At Studio Crave, brand strategy — what some call merkstrategie — covers positioning, target audience, differentiation, brand personality, values, messaging and tone of voice. It's the work behind Course 01 through Course 04 of the 7 Course Branding Experience.

Questions this answers

  • Who is this brand actually for, and who is it deliberately not for?
  • What makes this business different from the others your client is comparing?
  • What does the brand sound like, and what does it never sound like?
  • What does the brand stand for when nobody's watching?

Once the strategy is set, it becomes the brief for visual identity — so nothing gets designed on a hunch.
