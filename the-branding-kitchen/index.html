The Method

The Branding Kitchen

Your business has the ingredients. We create the recipe.

The Branding Kitchen is Studio Crave's proprietary approach to branding — a methodology built on one idea: a strong brand is made the same way a great dish is made.

Nothing gets invented from scratch. Your story, your expertise, your point of view and your existing audience are the ingredients. Our job is to develop the recipe, create the flavour, present the dish properly and launch it into the world — in that order, not the other way around.

Every client goes through the same connected journey: the 7 Course Branding Experience. Seven courses, one process, no step skipped.
