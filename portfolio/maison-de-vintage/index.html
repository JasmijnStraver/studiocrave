Noor — Curated vintage fashion label

Maison de Vintage

The Challenge

Noor's label had loyal customers but a visual identity that looked like every other secondhand shop on Instagram — nothing signalled that the pieces inside were curated, not just sourced.

The Strategy

We repositioned Maison de Vintage away from "vintage shop" and toward "curated archive" — a shift that let Noor charge for curation, not just for clothes.

The Positioning

Maison de Vintage is positioned as the label for people who want one exceptional piece, not a pile of options. Scarcity became the selling point, not a side effect.

The Voice

The brand voice borrows from hospitality: warm, precise, a little formal — closer to a concierge than a market stall.

The Visual Identity

Grand-hotel luxury: mahogany, gold and branded packaging, delivered from a bellboy cart rather than a shipping box.

The Shoot

We built a signature brand shoot around the packaging moment itself — the unboxing became as much a part of the brand as the product.

The Launch

The rebrand launched alongside a limited restock, timed so the new identity was the first thing returning customers saw.

The Result

The relaunch sold out within 48 hours, and customers now keep the packaging as part of the product.
