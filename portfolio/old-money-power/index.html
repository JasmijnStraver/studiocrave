Lina — Business strategist & leadership coach

Old Money Power

The Challenge

Lina's expertise was high-level, but her brand looked like every other coach's — soft colours, stock photography, nothing that matched the seniority of her clients.

The Strategy

We repositioned Lina around quiet confidence: a brand that doesn't perform expertise because it doesn't need to.

The Positioning

Old Money Power speaks to leaders who already have influence and want a coach who matches their register, not one who's trying to impress them.

The Voice

Understated, direct, unhurried — a voice that never oversells because it doesn't have to.

The Visual Identity

A white tailored suit, a vintage convertible and vineyard backdrops: visual cues borrowed from old wealth, not new hustle.

The Shoot

The brand shoot was built around stillness — Lina photographed at rest, never mid-pitch.

The Launch

The new brand rolled out through a single, quiet announcement rather than a launch campaign — consistent with the positioning itself.

The Result

Lina tripled her prices after launch and built a three-month waitlist within six weeks.

“Mijn klanten boeken nu zonder twijfel. Het merk doet het werk.”
