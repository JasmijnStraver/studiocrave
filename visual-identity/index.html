Visual Identity

Make your brand impossible to mistake.

Visual identity — logo, colour palette, typography, art direction and the graphic system that ties it together — is not decoration. It's the strategy, made visible.

Every visual decision at Studio Crave is derived from the work done in brand strategy first. A colour palette isn't picked because it's trending; it's picked because it says what the brand needs to say. The same goes for the huisstijl, image direction and social templates that come after it.

What's included

  • Logo and brand marks
  • Colour palette and typography
  • Art direction and image direction
  • Graphic elements and a usable brand guideline
  • Social templates built for daily use

The identity then carries into branding photography, so the brand looks the same in a photo as it does on a business card.
